origin

I spent three years at Bechtel Corporation, working up from Junior Software Engineer to Software Engineer — shipping production systems at enterprise scale, fixing what broke, building what didn't exist yet. It was steady, and it was going somewhere. But the parts of engineering I found myself most drawn to — AI, machine learning, the systems underneath the systems — weren't the parts my day job asked of me. So I left, and started again at TU Dresden. Not because anything failed, but because zero felt like the right place to rebuild from.
